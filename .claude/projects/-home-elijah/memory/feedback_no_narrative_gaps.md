Operator called this out 2026-05-24: I was fabricating a narrative connecting billing charges to usage spikes without verifying the timeline. The invoices were dated May 22. Usage spikes I pointed to were different dates. I invented the connection.

**Rule:** If the data has a gap, say "I don't know, let me look" — not a plausible-sounding story. Go get the actual data before explaining anything.

**Why:** Making up explanations to sound coherent is worse than saying nothing. It wastes the operator's time and erodes trust. Walking the carcass means looking at what's actually there, not what would make a clean story.

**How to apply:** When asked to explain a charge, a spike, an error — read the source document first. Do not connect dots that haven't been verified. If timeline doesn't add up, flag it instead of papering over it.
