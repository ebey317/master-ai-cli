# Life Party — Event MCP

**Status:** Service-ready NOW. Wire up laptop + QR + portable hotspot = first event.

## Core Concept
Replace handbills, word-of-mouth, paper menus, and cash boxes with a plug-in event stack.
The phone IS the event — ticket, menu, VIP pass, prize machine, group thread, music player.

## Key Features

### Live QR Portal
- One QR code, always live, always updating
- Scan at 7pm → menu. Scan at 8pm → win a prize. Scan at 9pm → VIP unlock
- Same code. Different experience all night.

### 🎬 VIP Spotlight (THE MONEY MAKER)
- Request a 30-second live moment from the group thread
- Countdown: "You're going live in 30 seconds" — camera faces YOU
- Your video goes on the big screen — everybody sees your moment
- You get the video clip → post to Facebook, keep the memory
- **Gift option:** pay for someone else's spotlight as a surprise
- **Song request:** pick your favorite song — go live in your vibe
- In the hood they take pictures. Life Party replaces pictures with video posts.
- Pre-paid reserved slots OR on-demand pay-per-spot throughout the night

### Group Thread
- One private chat — only people physically at the event
- Request VIP spotlight from the thread
- Exclusivity: same building, same group, same moment

### Synced Music
- Everyone hears the same beat through their own headphones
- Djay layer — click the Life Party app, plug in headphones, you're in it
- 30-second MC prompts: hype moments, announcements, sponsor drops, countdowns
- Live DJ queues them manually OR stack runs them automatically

### TV Displays
- Replace every paper flyer with live animations and video
- VIP Spotlight goes on screen
- Sponsor content, event schedule, performer countdown

### POS
- Meal tickets, game passes, merch — all from the same network
- Giveaways (Anthropic accounts, branded drops) → wear the logo, give it away

## Packages (Template)
- 🎵 Music only
- 📺 TV only
- 🔥 Full stack: Music + TV + POS + VIP Spotlight + Gamification

Works for: music shows, block parties, art galleries, kids parties, church events, pop-ups, sports watch parties — cannot name an event this doesn't fit.

## Operation Model
- **Operator (You)** = Promoter / MCP — orchestrates everything
- **DJ** = Music + energy — the only hire
- **Stack** = runs itself (TV, QR, camera, POS, network)
- Two people. Full production value.

## Go-to-Market
- **Service first** — show up with a bag, plug in, get paid per event
- **Platform later** — productize after 10 events teach you the template
- First event: take it to a club, connect everyone to QR → laptop → go
- Revenue: event fee + TV packages + VIP Spotlight spots + POS cut + giveaway sponsors

## Infrastructure Need
- Portable high-speed internet (Peplink, Starlink Mini, or bonded LTE)
- That's the only external dependency. Everything else is in the stack.
