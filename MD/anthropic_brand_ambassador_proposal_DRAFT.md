## How I got here

The first time I logged into Claude, I had one question. I was building an SKS camera system — a setup that points a camera at a pile of trash, junk, scrap, whatever, and tells you what you're looking at. Not generic "this is metal," but: what components are in it, what materials, what's recyclable, what's actually valuable, what you could pull out and use bushcraft-style or DIY-style for off-grid living. A camera that looks at a pile of trash and tells you you've got gold in there, not just trash.

I asked Claude that question expecting the answer most AIs give: a bullet list of obvious things, some hedging, a polite "you should consult an expert." Instead the answer Claude gave me made me realize I was on base 44 trying to build something a free copy of you could already do. I was paying for something a regular common guy could be doing. The gap wasn't capability. The gap was that nobody had told the common guy what was possible.

So I started drilling. I asked everything I could think of — what can you do, what can you make, what can you teach me, what can you help me build. Whatever it was, this wasn't a chatbox. This wasn't an HTML question-and-answer widget. This was something else.

My nephew had told me earlier that AI could do things I should explore — write a book, build an app, get curious. That conversation got me started. But it was Claude that turned a curiosity into a door. AI had been presented to me, the same way it's presented to most people, as a chatbox. **Claude presented itself to me as a tool that could be valuable in my actual life.**

## What I built

**Master AI** is a local-first AI agent stack designed for the customer's own machine. Two pieces ship:

- **Sensei** — a tmux-hosted terminal agent (the brain). Runs a local 7B model via Ollama by default, with optional cloud escalation per message to Anthropic's API (and others, BYOK). One-command terminal entry.
- **sensei_extension** — a Chrome MV3 extension (the hands). Provides browser automation surfaces — read, fill, click, navigate, upload — so the agent can operate inside the user's already-logged-in Chrome session. The icon is a puzzle piece by design.

The two pieces are linked through MCP (Model Context Protocol). The architecture is **brain + hands + wire + brand**, with MCP as the wire. Nothing about it requires the user to hand over credentials to a remote server. The user owns their own machine, their own keys, their own data. Sensitive form fields are gated behind a sensitivity tier system (`personal`, `financial`, `government_id`) with non-negotiable refusal at the top two tiers, regardless of match confidence. Every executor decision writes an append-only audit log. There are no silent decisions.

Master AI is currently at a v1-buyer-bundle stage. The packaging script (`pack_for_sale.sh`) produces a customer-installable zip, scrubbed of personal data, with a one-command install. The product is priced once, with no subscription — the buyer owns it forever, can upgrade hardware as they need to, and never has to renew. This is intentional, and I'll come back to why it matters in a moment.
